A simple repeatable workflow.

Build a short story one step at a time, using accessible tools and a consistent visual style.

STEP 01 / SCRIPT

Start with a human moment

Find a relatable emotion, relationship or life lesson. Shape it into a concise Telugu story with a clear opening and payoff.

STEP 02 / VISUALS + VOICE

Bring the story to life

Plan simple 2D cartoon scenes and a voiceover. Keep the characters expressive and the visuals focused on the story.

STEP 03 / EDIT + POST

Make it easy to watch

Put the scenes, voice and captions together in a vertical reel. Post consistently, then learn from how people respond.
